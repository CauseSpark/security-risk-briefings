# What an "ATO" means

> Plain-language explainer for system owners. Fictional company.

ATO stands for **Authorization to Operate**. It is a signed decision that a system is safe
enough to use.

## Who decides

One senior leader makes the call. At Acme, that is the CIO. The CIO is called the
Authorizing Official. The CIO does not decide alone. The CIO reads three things first:

1. **The security plan.** What the system is and how it is protected.
2. **The assessment report.** What an independent team tested and found.
3. **The POA&M.** The list of fixes still to do, with dates.

## What "safe enough" means

No system is perfect. The question is whether the risk that is left is one the company can
live with. The HR Portal got its ATO on November 2, 2026, with conditions. The main condition is
that the open fixes get done on time.

## An ATO is not the end

After the ATO, the system is watched all the time. Scans run every week. Logs are reviewed.
The fix list is updated every month. Once a year, we do a full review and the CIO looks again.
If something big changes, like a new vendor link, the CIO may need to decide again.

## What system owners do

- Fund and staff the fixes on the list.
- Tell the security officer before making big changes.
- Join the yearly review.
